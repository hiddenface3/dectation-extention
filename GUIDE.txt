🚀 CHATGPT, GROK & GEMINI DICTATION AUTOMATOR — USER GUIDE & SETUP MANUAL 🚀
================================================================================

Welcome! This extension lets you trigger dictation seamlessly on ChatGPT, Grok, 
Google Gemini, or ANY web page from anywhere using global keyboard shortcuts. 
Follow this simple guide to set everything up in less than 2 minutes! ✨

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📦 STEP 1: INSTALLING THE EXTENSION IN YOUR BROWSER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1️⃣ Open Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
2️⃣ In the address bar, type one of the following and press Enter:
   • Chrome: chrome://extensions
   • Edge:   edge://extensions
3️⃣ Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right corner toggle switch ⚙️).
4️⃣ Click the "Load unpacked" button (top-left).
5️⃣ Select the folder containing this extension (`DECTATION - for edge cpy date13`).
6️⃣ Done! 🎉 You will now see the "Dictation Automator Control" icon in your browser toolbar!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⌨️ STEP 2: VERIFYING YOUR GLOBAL SHORTCUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Default Shortcut:  Ctrl + Shift + Z  (Windows / Linux)
• What it does:      Press ONCE to Start Listening 🎙️
                     Press AGAIN to Stop & Copy/Paste 📝

Need to change the keyboard shortcut?
1️⃣ Go to `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` or `edge://extensions/shortcuts`.
2️⃣ Scroll to "ChatGPT Dictation Automator".
3️⃣ Click the pencil icon to set your preferred custom key combination!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎙️ STEP 3: HOW TO USE DICTATION (SUPPORTS CHATGPT, GROK, GEMINI & MORE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1️⃣ Open ChatGPT (`https://chatgpt.com`), Grok (`https://grok.com`), or Gemini (`https://gemini.google.com`).
2️⃣ Select your target service in the Extension Popup dropdown:
   • ChatGPT
   • Grok
   • Google Gemini
   • Current Active Tab (runs directly on whatever site is open)
3️⃣ Anywhere on your computer, press  Ctrl + Shift + Z .
4️⃣ The browser will instantly focus the tab and start voice recording!
5️⃣ Speak your prompt clearly into your microphone. 🗣️
6️⃣ Press  Ctrl + Shift + Z  again when finished speaking.
7️⃣ Your dictation text will be captured and copied to your clipboard! 📋

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🤖 STEP 4: ONE-CLICK AUTO-REPAIR PAGE BUTTON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If any AI site updates its layout and dictation stops working:

1️⃣ Open the AI site (ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, etc.).
2️⃣ Click the extension icon 🎙️ in your browser toolbar.
3️⃣ Click the blue button:  🤖 Auto-Repair Current Page .
4️⃣ The extension automatically scans the page's microphone button, text area, 
   and send button, updates settings, and saves them! ✅

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎯 STEP 5: FIXING BUTTONS VISUALLY (VISUAL ELEMENT PICKER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If you want to manually pick the exact microphone button:

1️⃣ Click the extension icon 🎙️ in your browser toolbar.
2️⃣ Click the green button:  🎯 Pick Mic Button Visually .
3️⃣ Your mouse cursor on the webpage will turn into an interactive picker!
4️⃣ Hover over the Microphone / Dictate button (a glowing teal box will follow).
5️⃣ Click the microphone button ONCE on screen.
6️⃣ Boom! ✅ A toast notification will say "Mic button saved!".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
❓ TROUBLESHOOTING & HELPFUL TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

❌ Problem: "Dictate button not found" toast appears.
👉 Fix: Click "🤖 Auto-Repair Current Page" or "🎯 Pick Mic Button Visually" in the popup.

❌ Problem: Dictation starts but text isn't pasting into your other app.
👉 Fix: Make sure "Auto-Paste (Browser)" or "Auto-Paste (Desktop)" is checked in the extension popup.
